YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

We take privacy seriously. This page explains how information is intended to be handled in this learning project.

!

Important: This is an educational demo, not a final legal privacy policy. Do not enter real personal information while practising.

Information shown in the demo

The registration form asks for a name, mobile number, ward and consent. In the workbook version, the form is processed locally in your browser and is not connected to a registration server.

How information should be used

  • To demonstrate a registration flow for community participation.
  • To associate a sample resident profile with a selected ward.
  • To illustrate how clear consent and data validation can be implemented.

Your choices and rights

A real service should clearly explain why personal data is collected, how long it is retained, who can access it, and how a person can request access, correction or deletion, or withdraw consent where applicable.

Contact and updates

This prototype does not yet have a live county contact point or backend. Add verified contact details and have the final notice reviewed before using the site with real residents.
